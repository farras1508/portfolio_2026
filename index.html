MSc dissertation, University of Hull, 2025

Climate transition metrics and green bond issuance

Does what's happening in the real economy, like solar build-out, EV sales and water stress, move green bond markets? I built a 2014–2024 country panel and ran a tournament of statistical, machine learning and deep learning models to forecast five climate sectors to 2035.

  • EV share, water access and buildings carbon intensity track issuance most closely
  • Water-resilience and circular-economy finance lag well behind the need
  • Eight model families compared; the best one differed by sector

BSc thesis, Telkom University, published August 2024

Network topology optimisation for resilience and congestion

A digital company's communication network of 190 nodes and 278 links was losing about 70% of its packets to congestion. I modelled it as a directed graph, used the Bellman-Ford algorithm to find optimal routes and betweenness centrality to find the bottlenecks, then simulated adding new links where they would help most.

  • Found nine highly congested nodes to target with new links
  • Two link scenarios cut packet loss by about 61% and 54.5%
  • Packet loss fell from around 70% to around 30%

Personal project, Power BI and Python

Sales performance dashboard

An end-to-end business analytics case on four years of retail orders (9,994 order lines, $2.3M in sales). I validated the data in Python, built a star-schema model with DAX measures, and designed a six-page Power BI dashboard that answers five questions on time trends, products, geography, customers and discounting.

Power BI DAX Star schema Python Pandas statsmodels SciPy
View project on GitHub Read the analysis notebook
What the data showed
  • Sales grew from $484K to $733K a year, and profit nearly doubled, from $49.5K to $93.4K
  • Technology and Office Supplies earned 93.6% of profit; Furniture's margin was only 2.5%, with Tables and Bookcases losing money
  • The West region made $108K in profit, against $40K in Central
  • Discounts of 30% or more turned margins negative; the link held after controlling for product, region, segment and quantity (regression with robust errors)
